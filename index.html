About

I am a PhD candidate in Financial Economics at the Faculty of Economics and Business, University of Groningen, supervised by Prof. Dr. Abe de Jong and Dr. Halit Gonenc. From October 2026 to January 2027, I will be a visiting PhD researcher at the Fanhai International School of Finance, Fudan University.

My research focuses on empirical corporate finance, with particular interests in state-owned enterprises, spillover effects under imperfect competition, and mergers and acquisitions, using evidence from Chinese financial markets.

Before my PhD, I obtained a Master of Business and Economics from KU Leuven and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from Murray State University. My PhD study is fully funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC).
